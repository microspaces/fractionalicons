05 / FIT

For leaders who need an owner, not an advisor.

The right engagement has executive sponsorship, meaningful data or automation risk, and a mandate to change how AI is governed and delivered.

Who is this designed for?

Founder-led, private-equity-backed, and mid-market companies with active AI initiatives—but without a senior operator accountable for strategy, security, and execution across the portfolio.

Why begin with security?

Because AI adoption changes the attack surface, the data boundary, and the decision chain at the same time. Security creates the operating constraints that let the rest of the portfolio move with confidence.

What is Agentic Defender?

It is an agentic security product created by Cyberchain and made available in ClawHub. The engagement can include planning and operational support around its use as part of a broader AI security program.

How does the retainer work?

Each engagement begins with a scoped security review. Ongoing work is structured as a premium monthly retainer with recurring billing and reserved strategy time once the engagement terms are approved.
